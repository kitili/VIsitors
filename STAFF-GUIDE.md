# Silverleaf Visitor Log — Staff Quick Guide

**Live site:** https://v-isitors.vercel.app

## Front desk (daily)

1. Open https://v-isitors.vercel.app
2. Click your campus card (e.g. **Usa River**)
3. Sign visitors in on the left form
4. Sign visitors out on the right “On site now” board
5. End of day → **Sign out all today**

## Visitor QR check-in

- Print the QR from **QR posters** tab or https://v-isitors.vercel.app/qr
- Visitors scan → fill form → done
- Visitors leaving → https://v-isitors.vercel.app/check-out

## History & reports

1. Open your campus → **History** tab
2. Use quick buttons: **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**
3. Filter by status, check-in type, or search name/phone/host
4. Click **Export** to download CSV
